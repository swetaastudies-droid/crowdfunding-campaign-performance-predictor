# 🥤 EcoSip Crowdfunding Campaign Performance Predictor

> **An AI-powered decision-support platform for evaluating crowdfunding campaign readiness, estimating campaign success probability, and generating pre-launch improvement recommendations.**

**EcoSip — Sip Smart. Live Sustainable.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **EcoSip Crowdfunding Campaign Performance Predictor** is an entrepreneurship and business analytics project designed to help startup founders evaluate the readiness of a crowdfunding campaign before launch.

The platform combines:

- Historical crowdfunding campaign analysis
- Machine learning-based success prediction
- Funding goal feasibility analysis
- Backer and traffic requirement analysis
- Campaign recommendation logic
- Pre-launch social-media readiness analysis
- Final launch-readiness assessment
- Interactive Streamlit dashboards

The project uses **EcoSip**, a proposed smart reusable temperature-control bottle, as the business case throughout the analysis.

The objective is not to guarantee campaign success, but to provide **evidence-based decision support** that helps entrepreneurs identify risks and improve campaign readiness before launch.

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Analyze historical crowdfunding campaign patterns.
2. Identify factors associated with campaign performance.
3. Develop a machine-learning model to estimate campaign success probability.
4. Evaluate funding-goal feasibility.
5. Estimate required backers and campaign traffic.
6. Analyze pre-launch audience readiness.
7. Evaluate social-media and marketing readiness.
8. Generate actionable campaign improvement recommendations.
9. Provide an interactive management dashboard.
10. Develop a final launch-readiness assessment for EcoSip.
